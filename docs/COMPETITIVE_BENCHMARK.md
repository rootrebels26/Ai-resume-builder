## 2. Key Competitive Advantages of ResumeStudio

1. **Zero Paywall & Frictionless First-Run Experience:**
   - Leading tools (Resume.io, Enhancv) let users spend 45 minutes building a resume, only to demand credit card details or lock downloads behind an \$8–\$29 paywall at the final step.
   - ResumeStudio allows users to create, audit, and download both PDF and Word (.docx) files completely free with no payment barriers.
2. **Local-First Privacy Guarantee:**
   - Security-conscious candidates, government employees, and defense contractors cannot upload their career histories to commercial cloud databases. ResumeStudio stores all data in browser IndexedDB, eliminating centralized data leaks.
3. **Transparent ATS Scoring:**
   - Rather than displaying an arbitrary, ungrounded number, ResumeStudio breaks scores down into:
     - Technical Stack & Keyword Coverage
     - Quantifiable Senior Impact (Google XYZ formula)
     - ATS Layout & Typography Compliance
     - Executive Role Alignment
4. **Interactive Checklist with Live Canvas Reversion:**
   - Candidates retain 100% control over which AI suggestions are accepted. Checking an item applies the senior-level improvement; unchecking it immediately restores the previous text.
